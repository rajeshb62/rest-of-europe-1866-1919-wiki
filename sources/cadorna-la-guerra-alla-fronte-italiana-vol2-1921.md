# Luigi Cadorna — *La guerra alla fronte italiana fino all'arresto sulla linea della Piave e del Grappa (24 maggio 1915 – 9 novembre 1917)*, Volume II

General Luigi Cadorna (1850–1928), Chief of Staff of the Italian army from July 1914 to November 1917, *La guerra alla fronte italiana fino all'arresto sulla linea della Piave e del Grappa (24 maggio 1915 – 9 novembre 1917)*, Volume II, Fratelli Treves, Milano, 1921 (Settimo migliaio). Copyright by General Luigi Cadorna, 1921. The text of this wiki is the HathiTrust full-text scan (University of Wisconsin copy), 298 pages.

This is Volume II, containing Chapters VII–XIII and the conclusion. Its dominant years are **autumn 1916 – November 1917**, from the autumn operations on the Carso to the arrest of the retreat on the Piave after Caporetto.

## Chapters

**Capitolo VII. Le operazioni militari nell'autunno del 1916** — the military operations of autumn 1916: the first, second, and third offensives on the Carso (the Seventh, Eighth, and Ninth Battles of the Isonzo), the offensive action on Monte Pasubio, the operations between the Avisio and the Vanoi-Cismon, and observations on the military events of the year 1916. **Capitolo VIII. Le operazioni militari nella primavera del 1917** — the military operations of spring 1917: the period before the offensive on the Giulia front, the offensive on the Giulia front in May 1917 (the Tenth Battle of the Isonzo), the Austrian counter-offensive, and the action on the Trentino front. **Capitolo IX. La battaglia fra l'Idria e il Timavo** (agosto–settembre 1917) — the battle between the Idria and the Timavo, August–September 1917 (the Eleventh Battle of the Isonzo): the dispositions given before the battle and the battle itself. **Capitolo X. Caporetto** — Caporetto, 24 October 1917: the preparations for meeting the Austro-German attack (Predisposizioni per fronteggiare l'attacco austro-tedesco) and the battle (La battaglia). **Capitolo XI. La ritirata fino alla linea del Tagliamento** — the retreat to the line of the Tagliamento. **Capitolo XII. La ritirata dal Tagliamento alla Piave** — the retreat from the Tagliamento to the Piave. **Capitolo XIII. La linea difensiva della Piave** — the defensive line of the Piave, the arrest of the retreat in November 1917.

The volume closes with a **Conclusione** (268). In the course of Chapter XII Cadorna discusses the mission of Marshal Foch in Italy (October–November 1917), rebutting the view that Foch came as adviser and inspirer of the retreat and arguing that Foch opposed it.
