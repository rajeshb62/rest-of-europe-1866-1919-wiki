# Giustino Fortunato — *Il Mezzogiorno e lo stato italiano. Discorsi politici*, Volume II

Giustino Fortunato (1848–1932), *Il Mezzogiorno e lo stato italiano. Discorsi politici (1880–1910)*, Seconda edizione, Vallecchi Editore, Firenze, 1926. This is Volume II, containing the final fourteen discourses (XX–XXXIII). The first edition was issued by Laterza of Bari in 1911; as the preface of the second edition notes, this edition omits the discourses for the inauguration of the railway lines from Rocchetta to Rionero and from Rionero to Potenza and the convivial greeting to Zanardelli at Melfi (26 September 1902), which appear in the author's own book on the Ofantine railways. The text of this wiki is the HathiTrust full-text scan (OCLC 7097481, University of Iowa copy, DG828 F6 v.2), 530 pages.

Dominant years in this volume are **1897–1910**, from the discourse on the Nineteenth Legislature and colonial policy (March 1897) to the discourse *Malaria e chinino* of 30 June 1910, the last in the collection.

## Discourses

**XX. La XIX Legislatura e la politica coloniale** (5 e 11 marzo 1897) — after the disaster of Adua and the fall of Crispi, Fortunato appeals to the electors of Melfi on the colonial question, urging an Eritrean policy of prudence and withdrawal. **XXI. L'ordinamento dell'Esercito** (4 maggio 1897) — the reorganisation of the army. **XXII. Per le lapidi a' martiri della patria** (20 settembre 1898) — for the tablets to the martyrs of the fatherland. **XXIII. Il dovere politico** (9 ottobre 1898) — political duty, with an appendix on the tributary burden of the Mezzogiorno before and after Unification. **XXIV. Per le lapidi ai Caduti di Adua** (6 maggio 1900) — for the tablets to the fallen of Adua. **XXV. Il regime parlamentare e la XX Legislatura** (13 e 31 maggio 1900, Melfi) — the parliamentary regime and the Twentieth Legislature. **XXVI. Dopo il misfatto** (11 ottobre 1900, Lavello) — after the regicide of Monza. **XXVII. Le spese militari** (23 marzo 1901) — the military expenses. **XXVIII. La XXI Legislatura e le pubbliche libertà** (18 e 30 ottobre 1904) — the Twenty-First Legislature and public liberties, with an appendix on the Southern Question and the tributary reform. **XXIX. In memoria di Giuseppe Plàstino** (31 maggio 1905) — in memory of Giuseppe Plastino. **XXX. Commemorazione di Emanuele Gianturco** (28 novembre 1907) — the commemoration of Emanuele Gianturco. **XXXI. I servizi pubblici e la XXII Legislatura** (10 febbraio e 7 marzo 1909) — the public services and the Twenty-Second Legislature, with a statistical appendix on the economy of Basilicata. **XXXII. L'emigrazione meridionale** (30 giugno 1909) — Southern emigration. **XXXIII. Malaria e chinino** (30 giugno 1910) — malaria and quinine, the closing discourse of the collection.
