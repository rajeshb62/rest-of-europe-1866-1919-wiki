# Tivaroni — L'Italia degli Italiani, Tomo II (1859–1866)

**Carlo Tivaroni (1843–1906)** — *Storia critica del risorgimento italiano. L'Italia degli Italiani*. Tomo II (1859–1866). — Torino: Roux Frassati e Co., 1896.

The second volume of the series-within-the-work bearing the title *L'Italia degli Italiani*. It carries the national story from the war of 1859 through the making of the Kingdom of Italy in 1860–1861 and the first five years of the constitutional monarchy, closing on the eve of the war of 1866. Where the first volume had surveyed the restored absolutisms and the constitutional Piedmont, this volume is taken up almost entirely with the armed unification of the peninsula and with the ministries that governed the new Kingdom after the death of Cavour.

Tivaroni divides the volume into eight *Parti* (Parte III through Parte X, the numbering continuing from the two *Parti* of the first volume). The opening Parti treat the war against Austria and the annexations of central Italy; the middle of the volume follows the expedition of the Thousand and the fall of the Bourbons; the closing Parti follow the successive ministries of the new Kingdom — Ricasoli, Rattazzi, Farini-Minghetti and Lamarmora — down to 1866.

The volume is approximately 500 pages of text, extracted from a University of California scan (Anna's Archive). It is the 506-page digitization.

## Content Summary

### Parte III — La guerra del 1859

The war of Italian independence against Austria.

- The secret meeting of **Plombières** between Cavour and Napoleon III (1858)
- The excitements and the plan for a European congress
- The battle of **Magenta** (4 June 1859)
- The battle of **Solferino and San Martino** (24 June 1859)
- **Villafranca** and the peace

### Parte IV — L'Italia Centrale

The revolutions and annexations of central Italy in 1859–1860.

- **Capitolo I — Le quattro rivoluzioni** — the risings in **Florence** (27 April 1859), **Modena**, **Parma** and **Bologna**
- **Capitolo II — Bettino Ricasoli e Luigi Carlo Farini** — the Tuscan and Emilian provisional governments
- **Capitolo III — Le Assemblee Costituenti** — the constituent assemblies of the central states
- **Capitolo IV — L'imbarazzo del Governo di Torino** — the embarrassment of the Piedmontese government
- **Capitolo V — La cessione di Savoia e di Nizza** — the cession of Savoy and Nice for the annexation of central Italy

### Parte V — L'anno decisivo

The decisive year of 1860: the expedition of the Thousand and the conquest of the south.

- **La spedizione dei Mille** — the Sicilian precursors; the decision; the landing at **Marsala**; **Calatafimi** (15 May 1860) and the astonishing capture of **Palermo** (27 May 1860); **Milazzo** (20 July 1860); the crossing of the Straits of Messina; Naples in 1859 and 1860; the invasion of the Marches; the battle of the **Volturno** (1–2 October 1860); the **Plebiscite** (21 October 1860); the capture of **Gaeta**; the conclusion
- **La morte del conte di Cavour** (6 June 1861) — the financial recklessness; Naples within the Kingdom of Italy and the *brigantaggio*; the negotiations of Cavour with the Holy See
- **Il primo Parlamento d'Italia** — the opening of the first Italian Parliament

### Parte VI

This *Parte* (its title is partly illegible in the OCR'd *Indice*) carries the early consolidation of the Kingdom through the first Parliament: the finances of the new state, the southern brigandage, and the negotiations with the Holy See over Rome.

### Parte VII — Il Ministero Ricasoli

The ministry of **Bettino Ricasoli** (12 June 1861 – 2 March 1862).

- The financial question
- The solicitations of Ricasoli to Napoleon and to the Pope
- The *Comitati di provvedimento*

### Parte VIII — Il Ministero Rattazzi

The ministry of **Urbano Rattazzi** (3 March – 1 December 1862).

- **Sarnico** — the Garibaldian rising in the north
- **Aspromonte** — the wounding and capture of Garibaldi

### Parte IX — Il Ministero Farini-Minghetti

The ministry of **Luigi Carlo Farini** and **Marco Minghetti** (9 December 1862 – 23 September 1864).

- The enquiry into the southern railways
- The **September Convention** (15 September 1864) with France over the Roman question

### Parte X — Il Ministero Lamarmora

The ministry of **Alfonso Lamarmora** (23 September 1864 – 20 July 1866).

- The armed bands in the Veneto
- The ratification of the Convention

**Note on the text:** The OCR is Italian. The printed *Indice* appears near the close of the volume and is partly garbled, so the structure above also draws on the running part-headers within the text. Tivaroni writes throughout from the perspective of the *Risorgimento*: the unification of the peninsula is treated as the culmination of the national movement, and the ministries after Cavour's death are judged by how far they advanced the remaining national goals of Venice and Rome. The volume ends on the eve of the war of 1866, which the third volume takes up.
