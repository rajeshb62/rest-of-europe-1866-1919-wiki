## Chapters

### Einundzwanzigstes Buch: Reform des Bundes (Reform of the Confederation)

- **1. Kapitel: Die neuen Zollvereinsverträge (New Zollverein treaties)** — The renegotiation of the Zollverein (Customs Union) treaties in the wake of Prussian hegemony: the economic integration of the annexed territories, the pressure on the southern German states to accept new terms, and the link between economic unity and political unification.
- **2. Kapitel: Befestigung der Zollvereinsfrage (Consolidation of the Zollverein question)** — The consolidation of the Customs Union as an instrument of Prussian policy, the Federal Customs Council (Zollbundesrat), the resistance of particularist interests in the south, and the interplay between tariff policy and the drive toward a unified German nation-state.

### Zweiundzwanzigstes Buch: Norddeutscher Bund (North German Confederation)

- **1. Kapitel: Preußisch-deutsche Politik. Anfänge (Prussian-German policy. Beginnings)** — The initial phase of Prussian statecraft under Bismarck after the war: the drafting of the North German Confederation constitution (the Augustenburg draft and Bismarck's revisions), the relationship between the Federal Presidency (Bundespräsidium) and the Federal Council (Bundesrat), and the balance of power between Prussia and the smaller north German states.
- **2. Kapitel: Italien und spanische Händel (Italy and Spanish affairs)** — Italian politics after the 1866 war: the acquisition of Venetia, internal instability in Italy, and the early stirrings of the Spanish question that would later culminate in the Hohenzollern candidature of 1870.
- **3. Kapitel: Deutsche und orientalische Frage (German and oriental question)** — The interaction between German unification and the Eastern Question: the Cretan Revolt (1866–1869) against Ottoman rule, the great-power rivalries in the Balkans, and Bismarck's emerging policy of limited engagement in oriental affairs.
- **4. Kapitel: Weitere Kapitel (Further chapters)** — Subsequent chapters covering the first session of the North German Reichstag (1867), the debates over the federal constitution, military budgets, the Indemnity Bill, the military conventions with the southern German states (Baden, Bavaria, Württemberg, Hesse-Darmstadt), and the consolidation of the new federal institutions.
