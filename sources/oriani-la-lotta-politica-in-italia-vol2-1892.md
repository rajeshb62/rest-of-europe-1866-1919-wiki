# Alfredo Oriani — *La lotta politica in Italia*, Volume II

Alfredo Oriani (1852–1909), *La lotta politica in Italia. Origini della lotta attuale (476–1887)*, Quinta edizione, curated by Achille Malavasi and Giuseppe Fumagalli (see the note to Volume I). This is Volume II, containing Libri IV–VI. The text of this wiki is the Project Gutenberg Italian eBook (EBook no. 43023, released 24 June 2013, prepared by Carlo Traverso, Claudio Paganelli, Barbara Magni and the Distributed Proofreading Team from Internet Archive page images), 203 pages per the source PDF.

Dominant years in this volume are **1821–1860**, the full arc of the Risorgimento from the revolutions of 1821 to the eve of the Expedition of the Thousand.

## Libro Quarto: Il Risorgimento

**Capitolo primo: I moti del 1821** — European influences; the Neapolitan revolution of 1820–21; the Piedmontese revolution of 1821; the absolutist repressions. **Capitolo secondo: Trame ed insurrezioni del '31** — the liberal incubation; the uniform conditions of the governments; the rising of the centre (Modena, the Papal States, Parma). **Capitolo terzo: Il pensiero politico nel moto letterario** — the first groups; the literary dualism; Colletta and Botta; Rosmini and Gioberti. **Capitolo quarto: Giuseppe Mazzini e la Giovine Italia** — Mazzini and the Young Italy. **Capitolo quinto: Conati ed imprese rivoluzionarie** — the expedition into Savoy (1834); the general state of the peninsula; the Bandiera brothers (1844); the reformers.

## Libro Quinto: L'ultima rivoluzione federale

**Capitolo primo: I prodromi** — the effervescence of opinion; Pius IX; the agitation in the other states; the constitutions (statuti) of 1848. **Capitolo secondo: Le sommosse popolari e la guerra regia** — the Five Days of Milan; the adhesions to the war; the Piedmontese campaign of 1848. **Capitolo terzo: La reazione federale** — the papal allocation; the betrayal of Ferdinand II; the annexations to Piedmont; the military disasters; the constitutional catastrophes; Pellegrino Rossi; the second Piedmontese campaign (1849). **Capitolo quarto: Schemi repubblicani** — Florence; the proclamation of the republic; the fall of the Roman Republic (with a section on Giuseppe Garibaldi); the last republic of Venice.

## Libro Sesto: L'egemonia piemontese

**Capitolo primo: Le ristorazioni** — the recovery of opinion; the Neapolitan kingdom; the Papal State; the Grand-Duchy and the duchies; the Lombardo-Veneto. **Capitolo secondo: La preparazione piemontese** — the first parliamentary difficulties; the Count of Cavour. **Capitolo terzo: La politica dell'egemonia** — Cavour's ministry; the Crimean War; the Congress of Paris; the adhesions to Piedmont. **Capitolo quarto: L'opposizione rivoluzionaria** — the defeat of Mazzinianism; Carlo Pisacane and the Sapri expedition (1857). **Capitolo quinto: La mediocrità politica e letteraria** — the decline of the national genius; the new schools; Luigi Carlo Farini.
