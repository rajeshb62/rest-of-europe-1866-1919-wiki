# Alfredo Oriani — *La lotta politica in Italia*, Volume I

Alfredo Oriani (1852–1909), *La lotta politica in Italia. Origini della lotta attuale (476–1887)*, Quinta edizione, curated by Achille Malavasi and Giuseppe Fumagalli, who adhered faithfully to the original Turin edition prepared by the author himself while also consulting the original manuscript kindly placed at their disposal by the author's son, Ugo Oriani. Three volumes; this is Volume I, containing Libri I–III. The text of this wiki is the Project Gutenberg Italian eBook (EBook no. 43022, released 24 June 2013, prepared by Carlo Traverso, Claudio Paganelli, Barbara Magni and the Distributed Proofreading Team from Internet Archive page images), 203 pages per the source PDF.

Dominant years in this volume are **476–c. 1825**, the long age of Italian particularism and foreign domination down to the reaction of the Holy Alliance.

## Libro Primo: Il federalismo municipale

**Capitolo primo: La fusione barbarica** — the fusion of Roman and barbarian worlds: the survival of Roman and Christian individuality in the federative structure of the empire, the foundation of the Lombard kingdom, the pact of Charlemagne and Frankish domination, and the catastrophe of the kingdom. **Capitolo secondo: I Comuni** — the origins of the communes: bishops and consuls, municipal wars, the descents of Frederick Barbarossa, the cities' war against the castles, the podestà, Guelfs and Ghibellines, the captain of the people, Ezzelino da Romano, Charles of Anjou, the tyrants, and Boniface VIII with Henry VII of Luxembourg. **Capitolo terzo: Le Signorie** — the rise of the signorie: their first attitudes, Robert of Naples and Bertrand du Pouget, Louis the Bavarian and John of Bohemia, the triumph of the signori, Cola di Rienzo, Cardinal Albornoz, the ideal Italian unity, Petrarch and Boccaccio. **Capitolo quarto: Venezia nella storia italiana** — Venice in Italian history. **Capitolo quinto: La rivoluzione militare** — Italy's military incapacity, the masnadieri (banditti), the condottieri, the effects of the military revolution in the republics, the triumph of the capitals, and its consequences for the rest of Italy. **Capitolo sesto: I principati** — the fifteenth century, the impossibility of a kingdom, Lodovico il Moro and Alexander Borgia, Leo X and Luther, Lodovico Ariosto and Niccolò Machiavelli, and the retrogradation of Italy.

## Libro Secondo: Gli Stati

**Capitolo primo: L'epoca della Riforma in Italia** — the age of the Reformation in Italy: spiritual conditions, political repercussions, the Jesuits, and the activity of Piedmont amid the decay of the other Italian states. **Capitolo secondo: La rinnovazione dello spirito nazionale** — Torquato Tasso, the political writers, Giordano Bruno and Tommaso Campanella, and the scientific emancipation. **Capitolo terzo: I regni del Piemonte e delle due Sicilie** — the century of Louis XIV, Venice, Genoa and Corsica, the other Italian principalities, Piedmont in the wars of succession, the Papacy, and the new Italian problem. **Capitolo quarto: Genio e carattere nazionale durante la formazione dei due regni** — the political schools, Giambattista Vico, Pietro Giannone, Metastasio and Goldoni. **Capitolo quinto: Il periodo delle riforme** — European influence, the two Sicilies, Parma and Piacenza, Tuscany, Lombardy, Piedmont, the Papal State, the suppression of the Jesuits, Giuseppe Parini and Vittorio Alfieri.

## Libro Terzo: La democrazia moderna

**Capitolo primo: Le repubbliche** — the French Revolution, the condition of the peninsula, the descent of Napoleon, the republican constitutions, the revolutionary settlement, and the Austro-Russian reaction. **Capitolo secondo: Fine delle repubbliche** — the French consulate, the battle of Marengo, the Concordat, and the Consulta of Lyon. **Capitolo terzo: I regni francesi in Italia** — the French empire, the fourth and fifth European coalitions (1807–1809), and the political changes in Italy. **Capitolo quarto: Caduta di Napoleone** — the Russian campaign, the catastrophe of the French kingdoms in Italy, the Hundred Days, and the writers of the French revolutionary and imperial age — Vincenzo Monti, Ugo Foscolo and the dialect poets. **Capitolo quinto: L'Italia sotto la reazione della santa alleanza** — the Treaty of Vienna, the Italian conditions, and liberalism under the Restoration.
