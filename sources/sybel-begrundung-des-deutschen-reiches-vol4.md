## Chapters

### Dreizehntes Buch: Spannung zwischen Österreich und Preußen (Tension between Austria and Prussia)

Covers the escalating tensions in the German Confederation following the Danish War (1864), the growing rivalry over the administration of Schleswig-Holstein, and the diplomatic manoeuvring that poisoned relations between the two great German powers.

### Vierzehntes Buch: Bruch zwischen Österreich und Preußen (Break between Austria and Prussia)

- **1. Kapitel: Allseitige Rüstung (Arming on all sides)** — Details the military preparations undertaken by both Austria and Prussia, including mobilisation schedules, troop concentrations, and financial measures for war.
- **2. Kapitel: Letzter Einigungsversuch (Napoleon's congress proposal, Gablenz mission)** — Napoleon III's proposal for a European congress to settle the German question peaceably, and the Gablenz mission as a last-ditch attempt at Austro-Prussian accommodation.
- **3. Kapitel: Österreich und Frankreich (Secret negotiations)** — The secret diplomatic exchanges between Vienna and Paris, including Austrian overtures for French neutrality or support, and the quid pro quo of territorial compensation.

### Fünfzehntes Buch: Preußisch-italienisches Bündnis (Prussian-Italian Alliance)

The negotiation and conclusion of the alliance between Prussia and the Kingdom of Italy (April 1866), the terms of the treaty, Bismarck's strategic calculations, and Italy's expectation of Venetia as the prize for joining the war against Austria.

### Sechzehntes Buch: Übereinkunft und Gastein (Agreement and Gastein Convention)

The Gastein Convention (August 1865) as a temporary expedient to partition administration of the Elbe Duchies, its terms, its reception by the German states and the European powers, and its ultimate failure to prevent the inevitable clash.
