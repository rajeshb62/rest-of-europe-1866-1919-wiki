## Chapters

### Siebzehntes Buch: Italien (The Italian theatre)

- **1. Kapitel: Die beiderseitigen Heere (Benedek, Austrian army at Olmütz, deficiencies)** — Analysis of the opposing armies: the Austrian Northern Army under Feldzeugmeister Ludwig von Benedek, its concentration at Olmütz, organisational problems, logistical deficiencies, and the command controversies that hampered its effectiveness.
- **2. Kapitel: Unterwerfung Norddeutschlands (Hanover, Langensalza)** — The Prussian campaign against the German allied states of the Confederation: the occupation of Hanover, Saxony, and Electoral Hesse; the Battle of Langensalza (27 June 1866) and the capitulation of the Hanoverian army.
- **3. Kapitel: Custoza (Italian defeat at Custoza)** — The Italian campaign: the Austrian victory under Archduke Albrecht at the Battle of Custoza (24 June 1866), the operational failures of the Italian army under General Alfonso La Marmora, and the strategic consequences for the Italian alliance.

### Achtzehntes Buch: Böhmischer Krieg (The Bohemian campaign)

The main theatre of war: the Prussian advance into Bohemia in three separate armies (Elbe, 1st, and 2nd Armies under Herwarth von Bittenfeld, Prince Friedrich Karl, and Crown Prince Friedrich Wilhelm), the battles of Gitschin (Jičín) and Münchengrätz, and the climactic Battle of Königgrätz/Sadowa (3 July 1866) — Moltke's masterpiece of concentration and the decisive defeat of the Austrian army, with detailed tactical analysis of the Prussian needle-gun advantage and Benedek's failed defence.

### Neunzehntes Buch: Unterhandlungen und Kämpfe (Negotiations and fighting)

The post-Königgrätz period: the armistice negotiations, the preliminary Peace of Nikolsburg, simultaneous military operations against the southern German states (Bavaria, Württemberg, Baden), the Prussian campaign on the Main, and the diplomatic interventions of Napoleon III as would-be mediator.

### Zwanzigstes Buch: Grundlagen des neuen Reichs (Foundations of the new Reich)

The Peace of Prague (23 August 1866) and its terms: the dissolution of the German Confederation, Austrian exclusion from Germany, Prussian annexation of Hanover, Hesse-Kassel, Nassau, and Frankfurt, the formation of the North German Confederation, and the constitutional groundwork for a unified German state under Prussian leadership.
