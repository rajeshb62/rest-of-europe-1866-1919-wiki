# Alfredo Oriani — *La lotta politica in Italia*, Volume III

Alfredo Oriani (1852–1909), *La lotta politica in Italia. Origini della lotta attuale (476–1887)*, Quinta edizione, curated by Achille Malavasi and Giuseppe Fumagalli (see the note to Volume I). This is Volume III, containing Libri VII–IX. The text of this wiki is the Project Gutenberg Italian eBook (EBook no. 43024, released 24 June 2013, prepared by Carlo Traverso, Claudio Paganelli, Barbara Magni and the Distributed Proofreading Team from Internet Archive page images), 203 pages per the source PDF.

Dominant years in this volume are **1859–1887**, from the eve of unification to the African adventure, the work closing on the battle of Dogali (24 January 1887) and Oriani's closing synthesis of Italian history.

## Libro Settimo: La rivoluzione unitaria

**Capitolo primo: La grande vigilia** — the exhaustion of the reaction; the last difficulties of Piedmont; the Napoleonic idea; the Franco-Sardinian alliance. **Capitolo secondo: La conquista regia** — the Franco-Sardinian war of 1859; the French peace; the catastrophe of the Piedmontese idea. **Capitolo terzo: Prime integrazioni rivoluzionarie** — the provisional governments in central Italy; dictatorial initiatives; the annexations of central Italy; the cession of Nice and Savoy. **Capitolo quarto: La conquista rivoluzionaria** — the Thousand of Marsala; the last resistances of the Bourbon and Piedmontese governments; the enterprise of Naples; the Piedmontese campaign in the Marches; the annexation of the kingdom.

## Libro Ottavo: Il regno d'Italia

**Capitolo primo: Il primo assetto** — the historical insufficiency of the new monarchy; the data of monarchical policy; parliamentary characters; political difficulties. **Capitolo secondo: La proclamazione di Roma capitale** — diplomatic negotiations; the Buoncompagni order of the day; the last struggle between Garibaldi and Cavour; the Regions. **Capitolo terzo: I luogotenenti di Cavour** — the political environment; the first ministries; legislative empiricism. **Capitolo quarto: La reazione del brigantaggio nel mezzogiorno** — the brigandage reaction in the South. **Capitolo quinto: La tragedia d'Aspromonte** — the adventure of Sarnico; the second Garibaldian expedition; the Italian monarchy and the Papacy. **Capitolo sesto: Soluzione monarchica del problema di Roma** — Rome during the revolution; the September Convention (1864); the transfer of the capital to Florence. **Capitolo settimo: La prima guerra italiana nel veneto** — royal and democratic conspiracies; the Prussian preparation; negotiations and equipment; the campaign of 1866; Garibaldi in the Tirol; the battle of Lissa; the Peace of Vienna. **Capitolo ottavo: Ultima ripresa rivoluzionaria** — the last brigand reaction at Palermo; ecclesiastical policy; the Rattazzi ministry; Mentana (1867); parliamentary reactions; the last Mazzinian attempts. **Capitolo nono: La crisi finanziaria** — the economic environment; Quintino Sella; the danger of bankruptcy. **Capitolo decimo: La presa di Roma** — the rivalry of France with Prussia; the end of the temporal power; the Franco-Prussian War; monarchical hesitations; the annexation of Rome (1870); the entry of Victor Emmanuel into Rome; Garibaldi in France; the Law of the Guarantees (1871). **Capitolo undecimo: L'opposizione ideale** — literary decadence; Giosuè Carducci.

## Libro Nono: Il secondo periodo monarchico

**Capitolo primo: Le due monarchie** — the exhaustion of the Right; the prisoners of the monarchy; the last Minghetti ministry; the advent of the Left (1876). **Capitolo secondo: La conquista africana** — the attraction of European history; European influence on Africa; the Italian initiative; diplomatic difficulties; the battle of Dogali (24 January 1887). **Capitolo terzo: L'Italia in Europa** — the closing synthesis of the whole work: Oriani's reflection, ranging back over the entire course of Italian history from the Etruscans and Rome through the barbarian invasions and the medieval and modern ages, on how the Italian nation, risen again and constituted, had taken its place again in Europe.
