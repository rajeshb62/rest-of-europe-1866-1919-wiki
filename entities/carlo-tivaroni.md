# Carlo Tivaroni

**Carlo Tivaroni** (1826–1898) was an Italian patriot and historian, author of the monumental *Storia critica del risorgimento italiano*, of which the *L'Italia degli Italiani* volumes in this corpus form the third series. A participant in the struggles he narrated, he brought to his history the memory of the revolutionary generation and the partisan viewpoint of the moderate national movement.

## A patriot historian

Born in 1826, Tivaroni belonged to the generation that made the Risorgimento. His *Storia critica*, published in the decades after unification, was one of the most ambitious contemporary histories of the movement, cast in a critical and narrative mode that sought to explain how Italy was made. The two volumes used here carry the story from the war of 1859 through the acquisition of the Veneto in 1866 and the taking of Rome in 1870.

## The two volumes

The second *Italia degli Italiani* volume (1859–1866) narrates the battles of Magenta, Solferino and Villafranca; the revolutions and annexations of central Italy and the cession of Savoy and Nice; the Expedition of the Thousand, the conquest of the Two Sicilies and the fall of Gaeta; the death of **Cavour** (6 June 1861); and the succession of ministries of the new Kingdom under Ricasoli, Rattazzi, Farini-Minghetti and Lamarmora. The third volume (1866–1870) covers the war of 1866 against Austria, the Roman question from the Syllabus through Mentana, and the breach of Porta Pia on 20 September 1870, closing with a retrospective on the national idea and portraits of the leading figures of the Risorgimento.

## Place in the corpus

For this knowledge base Tivaroni is the contemporary consolidator narrative of Italian unification — the moderate-national historian who frames the wars, annexations and ministries as the unfolding of the national idea. He complements the first-person account of **Garibaldi** and the philosophic synthesis of **Oriani**, and provides the constitutional and diplomatic detail of the new Kingdom's early years.
