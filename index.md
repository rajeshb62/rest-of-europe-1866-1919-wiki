# Rest of Europe 1866–1919 Wiki

A narrative-research knowledge base on continental European history from the Austro-Prussian War through the First World War — the unification of Germany and Italy, the Kaiserreich, the Third Republic, the Dual Monarchy, the Eastern Question, and the road to 1914. The United Kingdom is treated separately in its own wiki.

## Sources

### Sybel — *Die Begründung des Deutschen Reiches durch Wilhelm I.*

- **Vol. IV: 1865–1866** (3. unv. Aufl., 1890). The break between Austria and Prussia, the Gastein Convention, the Prussian-Italian alliance. This volume straddles 1865 and is also available in the [1800–1865 wiki](../Rest%20of%20Europe%201800-1865/wiki/index.md). [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol4.md)

- **Vol. V: 1866** (1889). The Austro-Prussian War — the Italian theatre (Custoza, Lissa), the Bohemian campaign (Königgrätz/Sadowa), the peace negotiations and the foundations of the new Reich. [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol5.md)

- **Vol. VI: 1866–1867** (1.–4. Aufl., 1889). The establishment of the North German Confederation, the Reichstag of 1867, the Zollverein reform, the military conventions with the southern states, and Prussian consolidation. [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol6.md)

- **Vol. VII: 1867–1870** (1894). The Luxembourg crisis, the fluctuations of French policy under Napoleon III, the first Zollparlament, the Hohenzollern candidature, the Ems Telegram, and the outbreak of the Franco-Prussian War. [Read source page](sources/sybel-begrundung-des-deutschen-reiches-vol7.md)
